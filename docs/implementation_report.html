2026-06-11 Backend pass — 1.0.3 gameplay updates

  • Cursed Player Head Drop: Changed to only drop (20% chance) when killed by another player (PvP only) via PlayerDeathEvent.
  • Ego Core: Blocked experience pickup via PlayerExpChangeEvent, and added Y-altitude damage backfire (1-2 hearts of damage when hitting a target at equal/higher altitude, showing Action Bar alerts and playing a tink sound).
  • Soulfire Core: Every 30s, 10% chance to set the player on fire for 5s, playing a pling sound and displaying an Action Bar message.
  • Sculk Core: Changed darkness instability check to in-inventory wide instead of only when held.
  • Core Placement Block: Added BlockPlaceEvent listener in SpecialItemListener to prevent placing custom cores (Ruined, Ego, Soulfire, Sculk, End, Blood) on the ground.
  • Core Crafting Timer: Set freeze stationary period to exactly 5 seconds (100 ticks).
  • Multi-Weapon Carry Restriction: Expanded the single-slot carry limit to prevent carrying more than one exclusive weapon (Maces, Spears/Tridents, or Cursed Swords) in the same inventory. Checked on pickup, inventory click, drag, and forge start.
  • Foot Particles: Added a high-frequency task running at holding-effect-tick-rate to display matching particle effects under the feet of players carrying any custom mace or core in their inventory.
  • Terminology & Docs: Updated wiki.html to replace "wielder" with "người dùng" or "người sở hữu", and synchronized all gameplay rules/instability descriptions.
